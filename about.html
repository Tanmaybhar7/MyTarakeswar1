About MyTarakeswar

MyTarakeswar is an independent digital tourism and information portal dedicated to Tarakeswar, Hooghly. Our mission is to provide accurate, easy-to-use navigation routes, temple history, dining options, and accommodation details to pilgrims and tourists visiting Baba Taraknath Temple.

Our Purpose & Services

  • Live Route Guidance: Instant GPS location and manual address routing directly to Taraknath Temple.
  • Complete History Chapter: Verified historical timelines, local lore, Dudhpukur, and temple traditions.
  • Dining Directory: Curated Veg & Non-Veg restaurant listings with phone numbers and map directions.
  • Hotels & Lodging: Verified hotel pricing, room types, and direct call booking.
